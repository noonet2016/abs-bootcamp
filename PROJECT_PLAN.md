# Project Plan — ABS Bootcamp

## Project premise
Create an educational experience centered on Sakon Nakhon’s “3 Dhamma”: Dhamma, nature, and culture. Initial source materials provide landmark descriptions, visual assets, and 20 route-based missions for an 8×8 grid.

## Scope (initial)
- Present the three learning domains and their landmarks.
- Support mission-based navigation on an 8×8 board.
- Represent four blocking obstacles: rock, tree, aggressive dog, and roadworks.
- Require players to collect a star where a mission specifies it.
- Use the provided Thai copy and PNG assets.

## Immediate next steps
1. Confirm the intended audience and delivery platform.
2. Agree the exact player actions and win/fail rules.
3. Reconcile every mission location with an available asset or identify missing assets.
4. Convert the source documents into a structured content inventory.

## Known risks
- Mission terminology is not fully consistent with the supplied image filenames.
- The requested product format and technical constraints are currently unknown.
- Source facts should be reviewed before public release.

## Definition of done (planning)
- Requirements, learning objectives, and constraints are confirmed.
- A complete content and asset mapping exists.
- The implementation slice and acceptance criteria are agreed.
